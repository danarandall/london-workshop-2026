# Before you come — 15 September, Cavendish Venues

Hi. You're booked into **AI for Accessibility and Cutting-Edge UX** with Dana Randall and David Franklin. Fifteen minutes of setup now will save you an hour of frustration in the room.

## 1. Bring a laptop you can actually install things on

We are going to install two browser extensions, sign into an AI builder, paste in files, and ship a working page. If your work laptop blocks browser extensions, blocks new sign-ins, or forces every download through IT approval, **bring a personal laptop instead**. This is a hands-on session. Watching someone else's screen is not the same workshop.

Chrome or Edge is easiest. Firefox works too, with a different colourblind extension listed below.

## 2. Create an account on one AI builder before you arrive

You only need one. Pick whichever you have not tried, or the one closest to what your team is already using. All four below have a free tier with enough room to iterate through both rounds.

- **Lovable** — [https://lovable.dev](https://lovable.dev)
- **Bolt** — [https://bolt.new](https://bolt.new)
- **v0 by Vercel** — [https://v0.app](https://v0.app)
- **Replit** — [https://replit.com](https://replit.com)

Already have a **Base44** account? Bring it. Dana will demo live on Base44 ([https://base44.com](https://base44.com)). The free tier there is tight (5 message credits a day, 25 a month), so only pick it as your build tool if you already have paid credits or you know your usage will be light.

Log in once at home so the sign-up flow, email verification, and any org SSO friction happens on your sofa, not in the room.

## 3. Install two browser extensions

Both are free. Both take under a minute.

- **Level Access extension** (Chrome/Edge) — automated WCAG scan with severity, element highlight, and code snippet. Install: [https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/level-access-extension/kgbmnemfaellbfabmkmmilchbhiigpdi](https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/level-access-extension/kgbmnemfaellbfabmkmmilchbhiigpdi)
- **Colourblindness simulator**
  - Chrome/Edge: **Colorblindly** — [https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/colorblindly/floniaahmccleoclneebhhmnjgdfijgg](https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/colorblindly/floniaahmccleoclneebhhmnjgdfijgg)
  - Firefox: **Let's Get Color Blind** — [https://addons.mozilla.org/en-US/firefox/addon/let-s-get-color-blind/](https://addons.mozilla.org/en-US/firefox/addon/let-s-get-color-blind/)
